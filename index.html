INKI ACE

Hold and drag to fly. Your guns do the rest. Refuel at gas stations in the sky, grab weapons in the air and take on 10 bosses in 100 stages.

COMING SOON TO THE APP STORE
HOW IT WORKS

FLY. REFUEL. SURVIVE.

FLYHold anywhere and drag. Your plane follows your finger and the guns fire on their own.
FUELYour tank drains as you fly. Catch fuel cans and drums, and fly under the Sky Pump balloon to refill.
LIVESThree hits and you're shot down. Armor plates soak a hit; double-tap to barrel roll through bullets.
WEAPONSShoot open crates in the air and catch the letter you want. Catch it again to power it up.
INK BOMBClears the sky when it gets crowded. Save it for when you need it.

Lift your finger and time slows down, then the game pauses until you're back.

WHAT'S INSIDE

A WHOLE SKY OF IT

100 STAGES

10 worlds from Harbor Bay to the Blot Citadel, with sandstorms, blizzards, lightning and lava. Three stars to earn on every stage.

10 BOSSES

Sky Fortress, Iron Serpent, Mega Zeppelin, The Inkwell and more. Shoot them apart piece by piece.

SPECIAL MISSIONS

Rescue pilots, bombing runs, escorts, recon photos, fuel rushes and Gauntlets.

25 AIRCRAFT

Propeller planes, helicopters, modern jets and future craft, plus a secret paper plane. Each one flies differently.

MAKE IT YOURS

Paints, wing emblems, contrails and extras like scarves, flags and tip tanks.

LIGHT OR DARK

Crisp black-and-white drawings that follow your device, or pick day or night.
